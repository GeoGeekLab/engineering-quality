# Code of Conduct

engineering-quality welcomes technical disagreement and expects contributors to keep it evidence-based, specific, and respectful.

## Expected conduct

Contributors should:

- discuss ideas, code, and observable behavior rather than attacking people;
- assume neither expertise nor bad intent from identity, background, or communication style;
- give concrete technical reasons for criticism and distinguish requirements from preferences;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and coordinated vulnerability disclosure;
- stop repeated unwanted personal contact or disruptive behavior when asked;
- keep discussions relevant enough for maintainers and contributors to make progress.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized harassment, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related interactions where a participant is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; close contributions; restrict participation; or report serious abuse to the hosting platform when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately when possible. Do not use a public issue to disclose another person's private information.

Enforcement decisions should be based on documented behavior in project scope, not disagreement with a contributor's technical position.

## Technical disagreement

Strong review is welcome. A finding should explain the failure mode, affected contract, or evidence gap. Disagreement about implementation style alone is not misconduct and is not grounds for moderation.
